Audio to MIDI, in the right key

Upload a recording. The notes are detected in your browser, then snapped to the scale you choose, so the MIDI file stays in key. Your audio is never uploaded to a server.

Note detection

Lower finds more note starts. Higher finds fewer.

Lower keeps quiet, held notes. Higher drops them.

One frame is about 11 ms.

Range and timing

Note timing in the file always matches the audio. Tempo sets the grid for quantizing.

Uses Spotify's open-source Basic Pitch model (Apache 2.0) running in your browser with TensorFlow.js. It handles chords and several instruments at once. Full mixes with drums and vocals are less accurate than single instruments or isolated stems.
